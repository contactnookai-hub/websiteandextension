FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else? Email us.

Is Nook really free?

Yes. One click answers, right-click Ask Nook and Answer whole page are free, with no card and no API key. Nook uses the Gemini in your Google account, so Gemini's own usage limits apply.

What data does Nook collect?

The free extension collects none. There's no Nook account, server or analytics in it; your question goes from your browser to Gemini in your own Google account. See the privacy policy.

Does it submit anything for me?

No. Nook fills in the answer and stops. You read it, change it if you want, and press Check or Submit yourself.

Is it always right?

No AI is right every time, especially with blurry pictures or tricky wording. Check every answer, and ask Gemini in the sidebar to explain it when you want to understand it.

What's Nook Pro, and when is it out?

A paid plan we're building, planned at $5/month: a better AI model, about 2 seconds for a whole page, up to 250 answers a day, shortcuts and Hidden mode. Press Get notified and we'll email you when it launches. The free plan isn't going away.

What's Hidden mode?

A Pro display mode: no bubble and not even the dots on the page, just a tiny loader by your cursor that turns into a check when the answers are in, then disappears. Double-click and the shortcuts still work.

Is it okay to use for school?

That depends on your school and your teacher. Nook is a homework and practice helper, and you're responsible for following your school's rules and checking every answer. See the Terms.

Can I change the shortcut?

Yes. It's ⌥⇧A on Mac and Alt+Shift+A on Windows. Change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
