Questions, answered

Is Nook free?

Yes. The free plan is everything the extension does today: one click answers, right-click Ask Nook and Answer whole page. No card and no API key. Nook uses the Gemini that comes with your Google account, so Gemini's own usage limits apply.

What's Nook Pro?

A paid plan we're building, planned at $5/month. It runs on a better AI model (99% right on 480 practice answers in 20 subjects in our tests), swaps the sidebar for a small bubble on the page, answers faster (about 1–2 seconds in our tests, and about 2 seconds for a whole page instead of 5–6), plans for up to 250 answers a day, and adds shortcuts, highlight-to-answer and a hidden mode that keeps Nook invisible on the page. The free plan isn't going away.

What's hidden (invisible) mode?

One of Pro's display modes. Normal shows the bubble and a dot beside each question, Dots shows only the dots, and Hidden makes Nook invisible: no bubble and not even the dots, just a tiny loader by your cursor that turns into a check when the answers are in, then disappears too. Double-click and the shortcuts work in every mode.

How do I get Pro?

It isn't out yet. Press Get notified and leave your name and email, and we'll reach out when it's ready. Pro will need a Nook account so we can tell who's subscribed. Nothing is charged until you choose to sign up.

What data does Nook collect?

The free extension collects none. There's no Nook account, server or analytics in it; your question goes from your browser to Gemini in your own Google account. See the privacy policy. We'll publish exactly how Pro handles data before it launches.

Does it submit anything for me?

No. Nook fills in the answer and stops. You read it, change it if you want, and press Check or Submit yourself.

Is it always right?

No AI is right every time, especially with blurry pictures or tricky wording. Check every answer, and ask Gemini in the sidebar to explain it when you want to understand it.

Is it okay to use for school?

That depends on your school and your teacher. Nook is a homework and practice helper, and you're responsible for following your school's rules and checking every answer. See the Terms.

Can I change the shortcut?

Yes. The free hotkey is ⌥⇧A on Mac and Alt+Shift+A on Windows. Change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
