1. What Nook is

Nook is a free browser extension that shows Google Gemini in Chrome’s side panel. When you ask it to, it reads the question on the page you are looking at, asks Gemini in your own Google account, and types the answer into the page. The free extension does not run its own AI. Its answers come from Gemini.

Nook Pro is an optional paid monthly subscription. Pro does not use your Gemini account. It sends your question to Nook’s server, which gets the answer from Anthropic’s Claude AI models. Section 8 covers Pro.
