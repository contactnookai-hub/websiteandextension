Privacy policy for Nook

Last updated November 15, 2026

Nook is a Chrome extension (“Nook | AI Answering Sidebar on Any Page”) and the website trynook.org. Nook is made by one independent developer, called “we” or “us” here. This policy explains what each part of Nook handles, who receives it, how long it is kept, and the choices you have.

The short version: the free extension sends nothing to us. The website counts visits without cookies, and only gets your name or email if you type them into a form. Nook Pro isn't available yet. Section 3 says how it will handle data when it launches.

1. The free extension

What stays on your device. Nook saves its settings, such as whether Auto fill and Auto copy are on and whether you have seen the intro, in Chrome’s local extension storage on your computer. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling Nook at chrome://extensions deletes them.

What goes to Google, and when. Nook only reads a page when you ask it to: when you press What’s the answer?, use the hotkey (Alt+Shift+A, or Option+Shift+A on Mac), choose Ask Nook on highlighted text, or press Answer whole page. Then it:

  • takes a screenshot of the visible tab, or of the pictures that are part of the questions;
  • reads the page’s title and site name, the question text (with math written out), the answer boxes and choices, any text you highlighted, and code in a code editor on the page;
  • puts that into the Gemini chat in the sidebar, which is your own gemini.google.com page.

Google receives it under your Google account, the same way it would if you pasted it into gemini.google.com yourself. Google’s Privacy Policy and the Gemini Apps Privacy Hub apply to it, and your Gemini activity settings at Google decide what Google keeps. Nook does not get a copy. Anything you type in the Gemini chat in the sidebar also goes to Google, from Google’s own page.

What Nook does with the answer. If Auto fill is on, Nook types the answer into the matching box or choice on that page. It never clicks Check, Submit or any other button. If Auto copy is on, or you press Copy, the answer goes to your clipboard.

What Nook does not do. The free extension has no Nook account, no Nook server, no ads and no analytics. It does not keep a list of the pages you visit and does not log your clicks, scrolling or keystrokes. It does not collect your name, email address, password, payment details or location. Signing in to Google happens on Google’s own sign-in page.

Permissions. Nook can run on the sites you visit so that the button, the hotkey and the right-click menu can screenshot the tab you are looking at, read the question on it, and type an answer into it. A separate rule lets gemini.google.com load inside the sidebar. That rule does not change other websites.

2. The website (trynook.org)

Visit counts. The website counts page views, how many people are on it right now, and clicks on buttons like “Add to Chrome”. It does this with its own small Cloudflare function. It sets no cookies. It keeps only the day, the page or button, and a random id that lives in that one browser tab (in session storage) and is gone when you close the tab. We do not store IP addresses, names or device details for these counts.

Hosting. The website runs on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes your IP address and browser information to deliver pages and protect the site from attacks, under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Fonts. Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts. To download them, your browser sends Google your IP address and basic browser information. Google says Google Fonts does not set cookies. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

“Get notified about Nook Pro”. If you use this form, we get the name and email address you type. The form is delivered by Web3Forms, a form-delivery service, which emails it to us. Web3Forms also receives your IP address, may run the message through a spam filter, and keeps submissions on its side under the Web3Forms Privacy Policy. We use your email only to tell you about Nook Pro: when it launches, its price, and changes to the plan. We don’t add you to other lists, sell it or share it. Every email we send will have a way to unsubscribe. We keep your entry until Pro launches and we have told you, and then delete it within 6 months unless you sign up for Pro. Ask any time and we delete it sooner. If you are under 13, please don’t use this form; a parent can sign up instead.

Uninstall feedback. When you uninstall the extension, Chrome opens a short page on this website asking why. Answering is optional. If you send it, the reason you picked, anything you typed, and the extension version are emailed to us through Web3Forms. Nothing else is sent. We use it only to improve Nook.

Email. If you email us, we keep the conversation as long as we need it to help you.

3. Nook Pro (when Pro launches)

Nook Pro is a paid plan we are building. It isn’t available yet, and none of this section is happening today. Before Pro launches we will update this section with the final details, and Pro will tell you what it sends before you turn it on. Pro will work differently from the free extension, because it uses a Nook server instead of your Gemini account.

  • Your account. Pro needs a Nook account so we know who has subscribed. We will keep your email address and your sign-in details, using a sign-in and database provider (we plan to use Supabase).
  • Payment. Payments are handled by Stripe. You type your card details into Stripe, not into Nook, and we never see your full card number. We get what we need to run your subscription, such as your name, email, country, the last four digits of your card, and whether your plan is active.
  • Your questions. When you ask Pro for an answer, the extension sends the question to Nook’s server: the same kinds of things listed in section 1, such as the page text, the answer choices, the text you highlighted, and a screenshot or pictures from the question. Our server sends it to Anthropic’s API (the Claude AI models) to get the answer and sends the answer back to your browser. Our server uses this content only to answer you and does not keep it after it answers, except in short-lived error logs that are deleted within 7 days.
  • Anthropic. Under Anthropic’s Commercial Terms, Anthropic does not train its models on what we send. Anthropic says it deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days, except that it can keep them for up to 2 years if they are flagged for breaking its Usage Policy, or longer when the law requires. See Anthropic’s data retention page.
  • Usage counts. We count how many answers your account uses each day, to apply the daily limit and to stop abuse.
  • Emails. We will send receipts, renewal reminders and account notices. Pro news is optional, and you can unsubscribe from it.
  • How long we keep it. We keep your account and subscription records while your account is open. After you close it, we delete them within 90 days, except records we must keep for taxes, payments or the law.

4. Who receives information

Only these companies, and only for the purposes above:

  • Google: Gemini, when you use the free extension, and Google Fonts on the website.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the website and the visit counter.
  • Web3Forms: delivers the website’s forms to our inbox.
  • When Pro launches: Stripe (payments), Anthropic (answers), our sign-in and database provider, and the service we use to send email. We will name each one here before launch.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone’s safety. If Nook is ever sold or moves to a company, this policy goes with it and we will tell you first. We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for advertising, and Nook shows no ads.

5. Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from the Nook extension complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The extension uses page content only to answer the question you asked and to place that answer. It is not used for ads, not sold, not used to decide creditworthiness or for lending, and no person reads it unless you send it to us yourself, for example in an email.

6. Children and teens

Nook is made for students, but not for children under 13. To use Gemini with a personal Google account, Google requires you to be at least 13 (or the age set in your country), unless a parent has turned Gemini on through Family Link. If you are under 18, use Nook only with a parent’s or guardian’s permission.

Children under 13 should not use the website forms and will not be able to create a Pro account. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without a parent’s consent, we delete it. Parents can email us to ask what we have about their child and to have it deleted. We never sell teens’ information or use it for ads.

7. Your choices and rights

  • The extension. Turn Auto fill and Auto copy on or off in the sidebar, or uninstall Nook. To see or delete what Google keeps from your Gemini chats, use your Gemini Apps Activity page.
  • What we hold. Email us to see, correct or delete your waitlist entry, your feedback, or (when Pro launches) your account. We answer within 30 days. We may need to confirm the request comes from you.
  • Emails. Use the unsubscribe link in any email, or reply and ask.
  • Where you live. Depending on where you live, such as California, another US state, the European Economic Area or the UK, the law may give you more rights, like getting a copy of your data, deleting it, correcting it, or complaining to your data protection authority. We honor these requests from everyone, wherever they live. We don’t sell or share data for ads, so there is nothing to opt out of.
  • EEA and UK visitors. We use your waitlist email because you agreed to it, and you can withdraw that at any time. Pro accounts are handled to provide the service you signed up for. The visit counter and security checks rely on our legitimate interest in running a safe website. Some of the companies above are outside your country, including in the US and India, and rely on their own legal safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, for these transfers.
  • Do Not Track. The website does not track you across other websites, and the visit counter works the same whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal. We don’t let ad networks or other third parties collect information about you over time on our site. Google Fonts and Cloudflare receive your IP address as described above.

8. Security

The website, the forms and (when Pro launches) the Nook server use HTTPS. We limit who can see any personal information to the person who runs Nook. No system is perfectly secure, so if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

9. Changes

When this policy changes, the “Last updated” date at the top changes too. If a change matters, for example new information collected or a new company receiving it, we will say so on the website and in the extension before it applies, and email Pro users and the waitlist. The extension will not start sending data somewhere new without asking you first.
