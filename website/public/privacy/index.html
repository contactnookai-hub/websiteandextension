Privacy policy for Nook

Last updated November 26, 2026

Nook is a Chrome extension (“Nook | AI Answering Sidebar on Any Page”), the paid Nook Pro subscription, and the website trynook.org. Nook is made by one independent developer, called “we” or “us” here. This policy explains what each part of Nook handles, who receives it, how long it is kept, and the choices you have.

The short version: the free extension sends nothing to us. It uses your own Gemini, and Nook collects no data from it. Nook Pro is different: to answer, it sends the question to our server, which gets the answer from Anthropic and does not keep the question. For Pro we keep your email, your subscription status and how many answers you use. The website counts visits without cookies, and only gets your name or email if you type them into a form.

1. The free extension

What stays on your device. Nook saves its settings, such as whether Auto fill and Auto copy are on and whether you have seen the intro, in Chrome’s local extension storage on your computer. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling Nook at chrome://extensions deletes them.

What goes to Google, and when. Nook only reads a page when you ask it to: when you press What’s the answer?, use the hotkey (Alt+Shift+A, or Option+Shift+A on Mac), choose Ask Nook on highlighted text, or press Answer whole page. Then it:

  • takes a screenshot of the visible tab, or of the pictures that are part of the questions;
  • reads the page’s title and site name, the question text (with math written out), the answer boxes and choices, any text you highlighted, and code in a code editor on the page;
  • puts that into the Gemini chat in the sidebar, which is your own gemini.google.com page.

Google receives it under your Google account, the same way it would if you pasted it into gemini.google.com yourself. Google’s Privacy Policy and the Gemini Apps Privacy Hub apply to it, and your Gemini activity settings at Google decide what Google keeps. Nook does not get a copy. Anything you type in the Gemini chat in the sidebar also goes to Google, from Google’s own page.

What Nook does with the answer. If Auto fill is on, Nook types the answer into the matching box or choice on that page. It never clicks Check, Submit or any other button. If Auto copy is on, or you press Copy, the answer goes to your clipboard.

What the free extension does not do. Without Pro, Nook has no Nook account, sends nothing to a Nook server, and has no ads and no analytics. It does not keep a list of the pages you visit and does not log your clicks, scrolling or keystrokes. It does not collect your name, email address, password, payment details or location. Signing in to Google happens on Google’s own sign-in page.

Permissions. Nook can run on the sites you visit so that the button, the hotkey and the right-click menu can screenshot the tab you are looking at, read the question on it, and type an answer into it. A separate rule lets gemini.google.com load inside the sidebar. That rule does not change other websites.

2. The website (trynook.org)

Visit counts. The website counts page views, how many people are on it right now, and clicks on buttons like “Add to Chrome”. It does this with its own small Cloudflare function. It sets no cookies. It keeps only the day, the page or button, and a random id that lives in that one browser tab (in session storage) and is gone when you close the tab. We do not store IP addresses, names or device details for these counts.

Hosting. The website runs on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes your IP address and browser information to deliver pages and protect the site from attacks, under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Fonts. Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts. To download them, your browser sends Google your IP address and basic browser information. Google says Google Fonts does not set cookies. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

“Get notified about Nook Pro”. If you use this form, we get the name and email address you type. The form is delivered by Web3Forms, a form-delivery service, which emails it to us. Web3Forms also receives your IP address, may run the message through a spam filter, and keeps submissions on its side under the Web3Forms Privacy Policy. We use your email only to tell you about Nook Pro: its launch, its price, and changes to the plan. We don’t add you to other lists, sell it or share it. Every email we send will have a way to unsubscribe. We delete waitlist entries within 6 months after Pro launches, unless you subscribe. Ask any time and we delete yours sooner. If you are under 13, please don’t use this form; a parent can sign up instead.

Uninstall feedback. When you uninstall the extension, Chrome opens a short page on this website asking why. Answering is optional. If you send it, the reason you picked, anything you typed, and the extension version are emailed to us through Web3Forms. Nothing else is sent. We use it only to improve Nook.

Email. If you email us, we keep the conversation as long as we need it to help you.

3. Nook Pro

Pro works differently from the free extension. It does not use your Gemini account. It uses Nook’s server at api.trynook.org, which runs on Cloudflare. Before Pro sends anything from a page, the extension tells you what it sends and asks you to agree.

Your questions. Pro only reads a page when you ask it to, by pressing a Nook button or shortcut. Then the extension sends Nook’s server the same kinds of things listed in section 1: the page’s title and site name, the question text, the answer choices, any text you highlighted, code in a code editor, and, when the question needs it, a screenshot of the tab or the question’s pictures. Our server checks your plan and your daily count, sends the question to Anthropic’s API (the Claude AI models) to get the answer, and passes the answer back to your browser. Our server does not keep the question or the answer. Our error logs don’t include question content, and Cloudflare deletes them within 7 days.

Anthropic. Under Anthropic’s Commercial Terms, Anthropic does not train its models on what we send. Anthropic says it deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days, except that it can keep them for up to 2 years if they are flagged for breaking its Usage Policy, or longer when the law requires. See Anthropic’s data retention page.

What our server stores. For each Pro account, our server stores:

  • the email address you used at checkout;
  • your Stripe customer and subscription IDs, your subscription status, and the date your paid month ends;
  • your private Pro key;
  • how many answers your account used each day, to apply the 250-a-day limit and stop abuse.

That is all. It does not store your questions, answers, page addresses, browsing history or card details. When the extension talks to our server, Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver the request and block attacks, but we don’t save it with your account.

On your device. The extension keeps your Pro key in Chrome’s local extension storage. Uninstalling Nook removes it from that device. Ask us and we will turn off your key and give you a new one.

Another computer. To use Pro on another computer, paste your Pro key in the extension. You can find it in your welcome email and on the Manage subscription page.

Payment. Payments are handled by Stripe. You type your card details into Stripe’s checkout page, not into Nook, and we never see your full card number. Stripe stores your billing details, such as your name, email, card details and country or postal code. Stripe tells our server your email, your customer and subscription IDs, and whether your plan is active, paid, past due, cancelled or disputed. In our Stripe dashboard we can also see what Stripe shows sellers, such as your name, country, card brand, the last four digits of your card, and your payment history. Stripe may use your information to prevent fraud, under its own privacy policy.

Emails. We send the emails the subscription needs: an acknowledgment with your Pro key when you subscribe, receipts, renewal reminders, and account notices. These go to your account email. We won’t send you news or offers unless you ask for them, and you can unsubscribe from those any time.

How long we keep it. We keep your account record while your subscription is active and for as long as needed after it ends to handle refunds, disputes and support. If you ask us to delete your account, or 90 days after your subscription ends, we delete your account record, Pro key and daily counts. We keep two kinds of records longer: payment and tax records for as long as the law requires, and the record that you agreed to the automatic renewal terms, which California law requires us to keep for at least 3 years, or 1 year after your subscription ends, whichever is longer. Stripe keeps its own records under its own policy.

4. Who receives information

Only these companies, and only for the purposes above:

  • Google: Gemini, when you use the free extension, and Google Fonts on the website.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the website, the visit counter, and the Pro server and its database.
  • Web3Forms: delivers the website’s forms (the Pro waitlist and uninstall feedback) to our inbox.
  • Stripe: processes Pro payments and runs the secure page where you change your card. Cancelling happens on Nook's own Manage subscription page.
  • Anthropic: writes Pro answers from the question content the server sends it.
  • Resend: our email provider. It sends Pro acknowledgments, reminders and account notices.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone’s safety. If Nook is ever sold or moves to a company, this policy goes with it and we will tell you first. We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for advertising, and Nook shows no ads.

5. Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from the Nook extension complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The extension uses page content only to answer the question you asked and to place that answer. In Pro, it is sent to our server and to Anthropic only for that purpose. Your account email, Pro key and daily counts are used only to run your subscription and apply the daily limit. None of it is used for ads, sold, used to train AI models, or used to decide creditworthiness or for lending. We don’t read your questions. A person sees them only if you send them to us yourself, for example in an email, or if Anthropic reviews content its systems flag for breaking its Usage Policy.

6. Children and teens

Nook is made for students, but not for children under 13. To use Gemini with a personal Google account, Google requires you to be at least 13 (or the age set in your country), unless a parent has turned Gemini on through Family Link. If you are under 18, use Nook only with a parent’s or guardian’s permission. To buy Pro you must be 18 or older, or have a parent’s or guardian’s permission. If a parent pays, it is their payment method.

Children under 13 should not use the website forms or Pro. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we delete it. Parents can email us to ask what we have about their child and to have it deleted. We never sell teens’ information or use it for ads.

7. Your choices and rights

  • The free extension. Turn Auto fill and Auto copy on or off in the sidebar, or uninstall Nook. To see or delete what Google keeps from your Gemini chats, use your Gemini Apps Activity page.
  • Pro. You can turn Pro off in the extension, or cancel your subscription with Manage subscription. Cancelling stops charges. It doesn’t delete your account record by itself; ask us and we will.
  • What we hold. Email us to see, correct or delete your waitlist entry, your feedback, or your Pro account. We answer within 30 days. We may need to confirm the request comes from you, for example by asking you to write from your account email.
  • Emails. Use the unsubscribe link in any optional email, or reply and ask. Receipts, your welcome email and renewal notices are part of the subscription and stop when your account is closed.
  • Where you live. Depending on where you live, such as California, another US state, the European Economic Area or the UK, the law may give you more rights, like getting a copy of your data, deleting it, correcting it, or complaining to your data protection authority. We honor these requests from everyone, wherever they live. We don’t sell or share data for ads, so there is nothing to opt out of.
  • EEA and UK visitors. We use your waitlist email because you agreed to it, and you can withdraw that at any time. Pro accounts and the questions you send through Pro are handled to provide the service you signed up for. The visit counter and security checks rely on our legitimate interest in running a safe website and service. Some of the companies above are outside your country, including in the US and India, and rely on their own legal safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, for these transfers.
  • Do Not Track. The website does not track you across other websites, and the visit counter works the same whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal. We don’t let ad networks or other third parties collect information about you over time on our site. Google Fonts and Cloudflare receive your IP address as described above.

8. Security

The website, the forms and the Pro server use HTTPS. Pro keys are long random values. The AI key stays on our server and is never in the extension. We limit who can see any personal information to the person who runs Nook. No system is perfectly secure, so if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

9. Changes

When this policy changes, the “Last updated” date at the top changes too. If a change matters, for example new information collected or a new company receiving it, we will say so on the website and in the extension before it applies, and email Pro subscribers and the waitlist. The extension will not start sending data somewhere new without asking you first.
